FAQ

  • How to reach - Town in the Rahata taluka of Ahmednagar district in the Maharashtra state of India.

    A.
    • By road - it is on Ahmednagar-Manmad road, NH 160. Nearest city is Ahmednagar – 80 kms & Nasik - 90kms apart.
      Maharashtra State Transport buses are available from major cities like Nashik, Mumbai, Aurangabad, Ahmednagar, Pune, Manmad and Kopargaon to Shirdi. Private air-conditioned buses also available from various cities of Maharashtra, Telangana, Gujrat, Andhra-Pradesh to Shirdi.
    • By Airport – - Shirdi can be reached from the new Shirdi airport (SAG) just 17 km away. Other nearest feasible airports pune is (201 kms) & Aurangabad is (112 kms).
    • By Train - Shirdi can be reached from the new railway station at Sainagar(at 4 Km distance); Manmad-Junction (at 61 Km distance), Kopargaon (at 17 Km distance) and Nagarsul are other nearby railway stations on the Central Railway.

  • Where to get VIP Darshan pass / Aarti passes?

    A.

    This services available on official website online.sai.org.in | www.sai.org.in and Sansthan official mobile app.
    At Shirdi, pass can be availed in person with manual process at PRO office .

  • Shri Saibaba Samadhi Temple is only in Shirdi ?

    A.

    Yes

  • Whether Saibaba Sansthan temple has affiliated or registered with other Sai temple ?

    A.

    No

  • Is there any offices of saibaba Sansthan outside shirdi ?

    A.

    Saibaba Sansthan has only one branch at Dadar, Mumbai.Information Centers are located at Chennai , Bangalore and Hyderabad.

  • whether saibaba Sansthan gives permission / approval to collect the donation to other agencies/ persons on behalf of saibaba Sansthan trust ?

    A.

    No

  • Which is the official site of saibaba Sansthan ?

    A.

    www.sai.org.in , Online.sai.org.in

  • whether Saibaba Sansthan had permitted anybody for paduka darshan / coins/ articles for Sai devotees.

    A.

    No. saibaba`s articles are displayed for sai devotees viewing in museum at Sansthan premises, shirdi .

  • whether Saibaba Sansthan had permitted any agencies/persons for booking of sansthan room & Darshan pass ?

    A.

    No.

  • Where is the first aid center in temple premises?

    A.

    The first Aid center is available near Gurusthan(butti wada),Darshan que and ground floor of Sai Niwas. Devotees are requested to get help of security guard for the same.

  • If any lost or found of any types of articles / cash/ pockets, whom to contact ?

    A.

    for any type of lost or found of any articles, cash or pockets, devotees are requested to contact our security department in temple complex. Contact no – 02423 -258888 / 5

  • If any missing of persons, whom to contact?

    A.

    please contact to announcement Centre near gate 03 or contact our security department in temple complex. Contact no – 02423 -258888 / 5

  • Where can I keep my baggage and other valuables while I am at the Temple Complex?

    A.

    Sansthan lockers are available at nominal charge.

  • Can I get any assistance upon arrival at the Bus-Stand?

    A.

    Yes, Reception Centre is available at S. T. Bus stand.

  • What is the schedule of Aarti during the day?

    A.

    There are four Aarti -

    • kakad Aarthi - at 5.15.AM
    • Madhana aarti – 12 noon.
    • Doop aarthi – sunset
    • Shej aarathi – 10:00PM

  • How can I make donation offerings to Shri Sai Baba?

    A.

    Offerings to Shri Sai Baba Sansthan are made in cash, Cheque , DD,MO or in-kind at Sansthan donation office. and on official website online.sai.org.in

  • Where can I obtain authorized books on Shri SaiBaba?

    A.

    All authorized Sansthan`s publications are available at book shops in sanstan premises, and on official website online.sai.org.in

  • Where can I buy Pooja articles before coming to the Samadhi Mandir?

    A.

    Devotees can obtain all the requisite Pooja articles at various shops on the periphery of the Temple Complex. However, As the various dealers and vendors of Pooja articles are not connected with the Shri Saibaba Sansthan Trust, devotees should first fix the price before buying these articles to avoid any trouble thereafter.

  • What are the timings of the Prasadalaya?

    A.

    Prasadalaya are open from 10:00AM to 10:00 PM

  • Are there any emergency medical services available at Shirdi?

    A.

    Yes, two hospitals are available 24 X 7. Contact no – 02423 -258666 /258555.

  • What is general timing of Sai Samadhi mandir Darshan que ?

    A.

    Darshan will open from 06:00 AM TO 9:30 PM .

  • Can I donate blood in temple premises?

    A.

    Yes.

  • Which are the important temples in shirdi to visit ?

    A.

    shri Sai Samadhi temple, Gurusthan, dwakarkamai, hanuman( maruti) temple, chavdi , three temples (Ganesha, Shanishawar ,Mahadev ), nanda deep, gurudev datta mandir are the temples to visit.
