# ShirdiYatra Evaluation Questions

These are 10 real questions a visitor would ask my ShirdiYatra app.
Q6 is a question my data cannot answer on purpose, to test that the app says "I don't know".

1. What time is the Kakad aarti at Shirdi?
2. What are the darshan timings for Shirdi Sai Baba temple?
3. How can a family of 7 reach Shirdi from Hyderabad by train?
4. Which railway station is nearest to Shirdi, and how far is it from the temple?
5. How much does a Sansthan room at Sai Bhakta Niwas cost?
6. Which private hotel near Shirdi has a swimming pool, and what is its price? **(NOT answerable from my data)**
7. What are the timings of the Madhyan (noon) aarti?
8. Can a single person book a room in Sansthan accommodation?
9. What is the cheapest room mix for 7 people staying 2 days?
10. What time is the Shej (night) aarti, and when does the temple close?
